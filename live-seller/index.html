Role Overview

As a Live Seller you will represent Eco Brand Japan during real-time shopping shows across social media platforms. You will share authentic product knowledge, cultivate a loyal community and translate livestream excitement into measurable sales alongside our operations and creative teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Host live selling sessions that educate, entertain and convert viewers into customers.
  • Present, explain and sell pre-loved luxury bags and accessories in clear, engaging English.
  • Showcase product details on camera, highlighting authenticity, condition and styling inspiration.
  • Build rapport with viewers by answering questions and fostering a welcoming chat environment.
  • Promote upcoming shows and develop strategies to grow your recurring audience.
  • Process orders in coordination with the operations team and assist with shipping preparation.
  • Collaborate with marketing on show formats, scripts and performance recaps to drive continuous improvement.

This overview reflects core responsibilities. Additional duties may evolve with business needs.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Fluent English communication skills with confidence speaking on camera.
  • Basic Japanese ability; hiragana and katakana reading skills are a plus.
  • Energetic, charismatic personality that thrives in live, fast-paced environments.
  • Knowledge of luxury fashion brands such as Louis Vuitton, Chanel or Gucci is an advantage.
  • No prior live selling experience required - comprehensive training is provided.
  • Valid Visa to work in Japan; domestic applicants only.

What We Offer

  • Full training delivered by experienced live sellers and luxury brand specialists.
  • Performance incentives on every sold item to reward outstanding shows.
  • An international, diverse team that values creativity, effort and collaboration.
  • Friendly company culture with a relaxed dress code - dyed hair, piercings and covered tattoos welcome.
  • Opportunities to grow alongside a fast-expanding global luxury resale brand.

What You Need to Know

  • Typical schedule: 5 hours per day, 3-5 days per week; probation period applies.
  • Eligibility: Working Holiday Visa holders are not eligible to apply.

Interview Process

  • Video conference (initial screen).
  • Video conference (follow-up discussion).
  • In-person interview.

Applicants living in Tokyo will be given priority. If you plan to relocate to the Greater Tokyo Area, please mention it in your cover letter.
