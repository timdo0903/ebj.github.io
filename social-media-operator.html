Social Media Operator

Plan and publish multi-platform content that strengthens Eco Brand Japan's voice, nurtures our community and supports global marketing campaigns.

Role Overview

As a Social Media Operator you will bring our luxury resale story to life across Instagram, TikTok, X and emerging channels. You will collaborate with creative teams to develop content that aligns with seasonal trends, supports product drops and grows an engaged international audience.

Key Responsibilities

  • Partner with marketing to build monthly and weekly content calendars covering multiple platforms.
  • Ensure every post reflects our brand voice, campaign goals and seasonal priorities.
  • Collaborate with photographers to produce short-form video, imagery and behind-the-scenes storytelling.
  • Create platform-ready assets including Reels, TikTok videos, product spotlights and story graphics.
  • Support editing and light graphic design to polish daily posts.
  • Publish content on schedule while maintaining quality and consistency.
  • Monitor engagement, respond to comments and nurture a vibrant community.
  • Track performance metrics such as reach, engagement, follower growth and conversions.
  • Report key insights, highlighting optimisation ideas for future campaigns.

This overview reflects core responsibilities. Additional duties may evolve with business needs.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Strong interest in fashion, luxury brands and global social trends.
  • Professional or internship experience in social media or content creation.
  • Working knowledge of video editing and design tools such as Canva, CapCut or similar applications.
  • Familiarity with major platforms including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and X/Twitter.
  • Detail-oriented, highly organised and comfortable juggling multiple deadlines.
  • Collaborative mindset with strong communication skills in English; Japanese proficiency is a plus.
  • Ability to work effectively with international, cross-functional partners.
  • Valid Visa to work in Japan

What We Offer

  • Exposure to an international luxury fashion e-commerce environment.
  • Hands-on experience across social media production and digital marketing.
  • Career progression opportunities within a fast-expanding business.
  • A creative, collaborative and multicultural team culture.
