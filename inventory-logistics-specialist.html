Job Summary

The Inventory & Logistics Specialist manages product movement from authentication through shipment. You will collaborate across teams to maintain accurate records, protect product quality and deliver exceptional customer experiences.

Authentication

  • Examine luxury handbags and accessories to verify authenticity.
  • Research and apply brand-specific standards to confirm legitimacy.
  • Stay current on counterfeit detection techniques and luxury trends.

Inventory Management

  • Record, track and update product information in the stock management system.
  • Conduct regular audits, troubleshooting discrepancies quickly.
  • Manage product flow, including returns, damaged goods and adjustments.
  • Maintain a clean, organised inventory space for efficient access.

Logistics Coordination

  • Coordinate daily product movement between departments, warehouses and shipping partners.
  • Prepare domestic and international shipments with accurate labels, packaging and documentation.
  • Monitor courier performance and resolve delivery or customs issues.
  • Support outgoing shipments alongside operations teams and stock managers.
  • Maintain shipping documentation, invoices and compliance records.
  • Track freight costs and contribute ideas for optimising spend.

Quality Control

  • Inspect incoming and outgoing products to meet company standards.
  • Partner with quality assurance to resolve listing or condition discrepancies.

Data Entry & Reporting

  • Maintain detailed records of inventory movement and shipments.
  • Generate reports on turnover, shipping volume and delivery accuracy.

Cross-Department Collaboration

  • Work with purchasing, sales and logistics to align stock availability with business needs.
  • Provide status updates to customer service regarding product availability and shipments.

Qualifications

  • Experience in inventory or logistics management, ideally in fashion or luxury goods.
  • Knowledge of major luxury brands and authentication fundamentals.
  • Meticulous organisation, accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in Excel and familiarity with inventory management tools.
  • Ability to prioritise multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment.
  • Fluent English communication; JLPT N3 or above is a plus.

Preferred Experience

  • Background in luxury consignment or resale.
  • Luxury authentication certifications.
  • Understanding of international shipping procedures and customs.

Physical Requirements

Comfortable standing, moving and lifting up to 10 kg as needed.
